Lurkify is an independent browser extension for browsing Twitch, YouTube and Kick channels, managing favorites and automating stream tabs according to your preferences. It is not an official product of these platforms and does not replace platform accounts or their terms.

3. Automation and control

Enabled Auto Lurker can open background tabs, change managed-player volume and quality settings, and close a managed tab when automatic closure is enabled. Optional Twitch Auto-Claim operates claim buttons. You can configure and disable automatic features in the settings.

View-count credit, Channel Points and Drops rewards are not guaranteed. Platform APIs, players, advertisements, browser restrictions and network failures can affect operation.
