Less manual tab management

Features depend on the APIs and player controls available on each platform.

Channels and favorites

Browse live channels, add custom channels and keep a separate favorites list. Twitch connections can import follows; YouTube connections can import subscriptions.

Auto Lurker

Automatically open favorite streams, limit managed tabs and set mute, player volume and quality preferences. Managed tabs can close after a confirmed stream ending.

Visible activity

Go-live notifications, player health, managed stream history and local diagnostic logs. Automatic actions can be switched off.

Why does Lurkify request YouTube access?

Optional Google sign-in requests the youtube.readonly permission. Lurkify uses it to read YouTube subscriptions and the channel profile, display those channels in the extension and check their streams.

This connection does not subscribe or unsubscribe you, upload videos or post comments. Public YouTube channels can also be configured with an API key. Twitch and Kick do not require Google sign-in.

Google data use and revoking access

Local preferences. Direct connections.

Preferences and operational data are stored in your browser. Cloud Sync is off by default and can optionally sync supported settings and favorites through Chrome Sync. There is no Lurkify proxy or separate Lurkify user-account server.

Playback is provided by the platforms; browsers and platforms may restrict the availability of features. Lurkify does not guarantee view-count credit, Drops rewards or identical quality options on every stream. Each platform's own terms of service still apply.
